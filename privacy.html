Tile Meadow Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

The short version

Tile Meadow doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account to create. Leaderboards and achievements are optional and run through Apple's Game Center.

What stays on your device

Your map progress, stars, settings, boost balance and games in progress are saved only on your device. They're never sent to me or anyone else, and deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards under your Game Center name. That's handled by Apple; I don't receive it. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app at any time.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your name, email or payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Tile Meadow is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email tiledoku.app@outlook.com.
